### **Notes on subscription billing cycles**

While the "Total Months" column reflects the number of *calendar* months containing a broadcast, strategic timing can reduce the number of monthly billing cycles you actually have to pay for:

* **Netflix:** You only need **3 billing cycles**. By subscribing for one month on September 10 (covering the Australia game), you can pause your subscription in October. Resubscribing on November 25 (Thanksgiving Eve) will carry you through to December 25 (Christmas). A final renewal covers the January 9 finale.  
* **Paramount+ & FOX One/Live TV Streamer:** You only need **1 billing cycle** for each. Subscribing on November 26 (Thanksgiving Day) will remain active through December 26, effectively covering both the Thanksgiving and mid-December Saturday broadcasts under a single month's payment.  
* **Peacock, ESPN+, & Prime Video:** Because their games run continuously every single week of their respective broadcast spans, you will need **4 billing cycles** for each of these core services to cover their schedules from September through early January.
